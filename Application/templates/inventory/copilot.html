SAFETY BOUNDARIES

  • All results use the application's existing inventory.view_asset permission.
  • The copilot is read-only in this release; it cannot reassign assets, approve disposal, or certify sanitisation.
  • LLM output is limited to validated search intents. It cannot access SQL, tools, application credentials, or remote device actions.
  • Only enable an LLM endpoint after organizational data-sharing approval. Do not include secrets or sensitive personal details in questions.
  • Any future write action must go through a server-side policy check and an authorised human approval workflow.
