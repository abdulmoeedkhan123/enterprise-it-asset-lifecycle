# Indicative Delivery Roadmap

> **Illustrative sequencing only:** Durations are relative phase placeholders, not an approved plan or commitment. Estimates require scope, capacity, procurement, architecture, and assurance discovery.

| Phase | Indicative sequence | Key work | Exit evidence / decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Mobilise | Before discovery | Sponsor, product owner, SMEs, boundaries, access, privacy/security contacts | Approved discovery brief and decision log |
| 1. Discover and baseline | Weeks 1-2 (illustrative) | Interviews, current-state walkthrough, data profiling, policy and source review, baseline measures | Validated problem, stakeholder map, source inventory, risks, measurable baseline plan |
| 2. Define and prioritise | Weeks 3-4 (illustrative) | To-be process, requirements, rules, roles, reporting definitions, non-functional needs | Owner-approved scope, traceable backlog, control/privacy review |
| 3. Prototype and validate | Weeks 5-6 (illustrative) | Dashboard usability, synthetic/approved masked data, scenario testing, metric reconciliation | User feedback, updated requirements, accessibility and data findings |
| 4. Feasibility and pilot design | Weeks 7-8 (illustrative) | Architecture, integration options, data migration plan, support model, costs and benefits | Go/no-go pilot case, design decisions, estimates and control approvals |
| 5. Configure and test | To be estimated after approval | Build/configure chosen platform, migrate, integrate, train, test | SIT/UAT evidence, security/privacy sign-off, operational readiness |
| 6. Pilot and evaluate | To be agreed | Limited rollout, monitor quality/control measures, manage feedback | Measured outcomes against baseline and decision to iterate/scale/stop |

## Delivery controls

- Maintain one backlog linking each requirement to process, design, acceptance criteria, test evidence, and owner.
- Use decision and change logs; document impacts across data, policy, training, reports, and controls.
- Agree entry/exit criteria for each phase and explicitly record unresolved assumptions.
- Do not present simulated dashboard findings as baseline evidence or pilot outcomes.
- Hold a scale decision until benefit measures, residual risks, operational ownership, and support are reviewed.

## Example phase gates

1. **Discovery exit:** Source owners and process participants validate current-state facts and data-quality findings.
2. **Design exit:** Business rules, target workflows, privacy/security controls, and metric definitions have accountable owners.
3. **Pilot readiness:** Migration/reconciliation, test evidence, training, support, access, and rollback are approved.
4. **Scale decision:** Pilot measures are compared to an agreed baseline, with limitations and confidence stated.
